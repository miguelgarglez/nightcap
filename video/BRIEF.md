## Product
nightcap — a caffeine planner. Drag your drinks onto a vertical day timeline,
pick your bedtime and liver speed, and meet the ghost still awake at bedtime.
Residual caffeine is computed with per-drink half-lives; the score materializes
as a responsive apparition. State lives in the URL hash; a share card exports
the night's verdict.

## Intent
Showcase / mini-demo in the style of the first-run guide. Hook: the ghost is
already awake. Beats: (1) hook — haunted score + ghost, (2) drop a drink —
amber preview trail, (3) it lands — trail feeds the ghost, score ticks up,
(4) drag bedtime earlier — the haunt deepens, (5) payoff — ghost at 100% with
verdict, (6) URL card.

## Audience
People who recognize 3pm coffee regret at 1am. Post goes to X.

## Voice
No narration. On-screen text in product type (Almendra display, IBM Plex Mono
labels). Silent video — `music: none`.

## Brand
Deep night navy (#0d1120 range), bone #f2ebdc, amber #f0a95c for proposed
inputs, spectral mint #a9e8dc for results, violet #6c5ce7 for bedtime.
Fonts: Almendra (display), Instrument Sans (UI), IBM Plex Mono (labels).
Motion: springy drops, dotted trails converging on the ghost, mist at the
bed line, fireflies.
